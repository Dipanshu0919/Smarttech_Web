About Interactive QR Code

Interactive QR Code is a smart, paperless governance and tax management platform designed specifically for Grampanchayats. It modernizes rural administration by replacing traditional paper ledgers, manual record-keeping, and long queues with a transparent, doorstep-accessible digital ecosystem.

The platform provides an end-to-end workflow that enables local administrations to efficiently manage tax collections, citizen services, payment verification, and village records while giving residents instant access to essential information through QR technology.

How It Works

🏛️ Custom Village Portals

Every Grampanchayat receives a dedicated and secure web portal customized with village-specific data, tax records, service requests, and administrative tools.

🏠 Doorstep QR Codes

A unique, weather-resistant QR code sticker is generated for every registered household and placed near the front door.

📱 Instant Citizen Access

Citizens can scan the QR code using any smartphone camera to instantly view tax records, pending balances, payment history, and property details without downloading any application.

✅ Centralized Administration

Officials can monitor collections, manage village records, verify payments, process requests, and generate reports from a secure dashboard.

Key Platform Features

1. Comprehensive Multi-Tax Ledger

Maintains detailed automated tax records categorized into Arrears (मागील), Current (चालू), and Total (एकूण) balances across:

  • Property Tax (घरपट्टी)
  • Street Lighting Tax (दिवाबत्ती)
  • Health & Sanitation Tax (आरोग्य)
  • Water Tax (पाणीपट्टी)

2. Bulk Data & Record Management

  • CSV Bulk Upload for complete village registries
  • One-Click Export for backups and Gram Sabha audits
  • Add, Edit and Delete household records
  • Centralized property and taxpayer management

3. Payment Verification Workflow

  • Status filtering (Pending, Paid, Approved)
  • Outstanding amount tracking
  • Payment proof verification
  • Instant approval and ledger updates

4. Official Service & Certificate Requests (सेवा विनंती)

Citizens can apply online for important certificates while officials can review, track, and digitally approve requests.

  • Birth Certificate (जन्म दाखला)
  • Death Certificate (मृत्यू दाखला)
  • Marriage Certificate (विवाह दाखला)
  • Below Poverty Line Certificate (दारिद्र्य रेखेखाली दाखला)
  • No Dues Certificate (थकबाकी नसल्याचा दाखला)

5. Bulk QR Generation & Printing

Generate and print custom QR code passes for every household efficiently, simplifying distribution for Gram Sevaks and village representatives.

Our Vision

To drive grass-roots rural development by transforming traditional village administration into a transparent, digital-first ecosystem that reduces paperwork, improves accountability, saves valuable administrative time, and delivers essential government services directly to citizens' doorsteps.

PRO: Complete Smart-School Ecosystem
High budget. Everything in Advanced, plus automation, AI, student access, fingerprint attendance, bus GPS tracking, school management tools, full legal compliance, and an inclusive experience for all families.

Tabs:
Common (on every screen)
AI chatbot: A chat icon on every screen opens a chatbot inside the app that answers common questions 24/7. Example: Parent types: "What is the fee for Class 5?" Bot replies: "The annual fee for Class 5 is ₹85,000. For a detailed breakdown, visit our Fee Structure screen or WhatsApp us." This reduces after-hours calls to your office.
Multi-language support: If your school serves families who speak Hindi, Marathi, Tamil, Bengali, etc., add a language switcher inside the app. Even just the admission form and fee structure in a second language goes a long way.
Accessibility: Make sure the app works for people with disabilities:
Screen reader support (TalkBack on Android) with descriptions on all images and buttons
Proper color contrast (not light gray text on white background)
Text that scales with the phone's font size setting
Captions on videos

Parent Portal: extra features
Late fee calculation: Late fee is calculated automatically on pending fees and shown in the fee statement.
Teacher's notes: Especially for younger classes (Class 1-8). Like "Rahul was very active in class today" or "Please send the science project by Friday."
Secure messaging with teachers: Timestamped, so there's a record.
Settings > Delete my account and data: Right to erasure. If a parent asks to delete their data, the school must do it. This option lets the parent send the request from inside the app, and it goes through a proper workflow in the admin panel.
Student Portal
My attendance, homework, and marks: Students can view their own records.
Study materials: Access study materials if you have a learning platform.
Staff/Teacher Portal: extra features
Communicate with parents: Message parents directly from the app.
Fingerprint attendance (view and correct): Teachers see the attendance recorded by the fingerprint system for their class and can correct mistakes (for example, a student who forgot to scan).
Promote to next class: Class teachers can prepare the promotion list for their class (see School Management below).
Fingerprint Attendance System
Fingerprint scanner at school: Fingerprint devices are installed at the school gate or in classrooms. Students and staff scan their fingerprint to mark attendance.
Auto-sync with the app: Attendance goes to the app automatically as present, absent, or late. No manual marking by the teacher is needed.
Instant alert to parents: The parent gets a notification, for example "Rahul reached school at 8:05 AM" or "Rahul is absent today."
Reports: The admin and class teachers can see daily, weekly, and monthly attendance reports by class, and export them.
Promote to Next Class System
Select students with checkboxes: At the end of the year, the admin or class teacher opens a class and sees the list of all students with a checkbox next to each name. There is also a "Select all" option. Untick the students who should not be promoted (for example, those who failed or are detained).
Choose the new class and section: After selecting, choose the class and section the students are moving to, and confirm.
Records are kept: The previous year's attendance, marks, and report cards are saved in an archive, and the student starts the new year with a fresh record. Fee dues carry forward.
Undo option: A promotion can be reversed within a few days if a mistake is made.
Inventory
Track school items and stock: Furniture, lab equipment, books, uniforms, stationery, sports items, and other assets. Each item has a name, quantity, location, and condition.
Issue and return: Record who took an item and when it was returned. Example: sports kit given to a teacher, lab equipment given to a class.
Low stock alerts: The admin gets an alert when an item falls below the minimum quantity, so it can be bought in time.
Purchase records: Keep a record of purchases (item, quantity, date, supplier).
8. Contact Tab
Tender / procurement notices: If your school publishes any procurement or tender notices, keep them on a dedicated screen.
9. School Bus (New)
Interactive bus route map
An interactive map inside the app showing bus routes, stops, and timings. This is a huge plus, because transport is one of the most asked questions by parents.
Transportation and Tracking System (GPS)
Live bus tracking: Parents see their child's bus moving on the map in real time, with the expected arrival time at their stop.
Bus-based login: Every bus has its own login (bus number and password or OTP). The driver or conductor logs in on a phone kept in the bus. While logged in, the phone sends the bus's GPS location to the app. Each bus sees only its own route and students.
Trip alerts to parents: Parents get a notification such as "Bus has started", "Bus is 5 minutes away from your stop", and "Bus has reached school."
Route and student assignment: The admin assigns every student to a bus and a stop from the admin panel. Parents see only their own child's bus.
Admin view: The school office sees all buses on one map, and which ones are running, late, or stopped.

Safety, Privacy & Legal
Full DPDP Act 2023 compliance: On top of consent management from Advanced:
Right to erasure: if a parent asks to delete their data, you must do it, with a proper workflow (including a "delete my account and data" option inside the app)
Data processor disclosure: if you share data with third parties (like a fee payment gateway, WhatsApp provider, or SMS provider), disclose it
Backend
App analytics: A free tool like Firebase Analytics. It tells you:
How many people installed and use the app
Which screens they viewed
Where they dropped off in the admission form
What device they used (phone/tablet)
This helps you improve the app based on real data.

Notes
Fingerprint scanners and the GPS phones or devices for buses are hardware. Their cost is not included in the app price, so quote it separately.
Fingerprints are sensitive data, and the data of children needs the parent's consent under the DPDP Act. Take written consent from parents before enrolling a student's fingerprint, and state it in the privacy policy.
Live GPS tracking runs continuously, so it increases the monthly server cost. Price it separately.
Android's bottom bar shows 5 tabs at most. In Pro, keep Home, Updates, Portal, and Transport as bottom tabs, with the rest under "More". The Home quick-action buttons already link to Apply Now, so Admissions can sit under "More".
